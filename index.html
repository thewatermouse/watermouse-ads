Fit

  • Selling in one or more EU countries

Not a fit yet

Questions

What people ask before signing

Do you handle my ad spend?

No. You pay Meta directly with your own card. Our fee is a flat monthly subscription and never a percentage of spend, so we have no reason to push your budget up.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Business Manager as a partner. If you leave, every campaign, audience and ad stays in your account, and you keep the ads we made for you.

Is it just AI?

No. AI does the daily work: launching, pausing, moving budget, drafting creative and writing reports. A named senior media buyer sets the strategy and rules, reviews the work and answers your questions. You always know who's responsible.

Do you guarantee results?

No one honest can guarantee ROAS. Results depend on your product, price, site and season. We guarantee the work: a set number of new ads tested each month, a weekly report and replies within one business day.

Which platforms do you run?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) for now. Google and TikTok come next, starting with Full Marketing clients.

Is there a contract?

Three months to start, because ads need time to learn what works. After that it's month to month with 30 days' notice.
